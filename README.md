# E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an **end-to-end E-Commerce Sales Analysis** built using **Power BI**.  
The dashboard provides insights into **sales performance, profitability, shipping costs, quantity sold, markets, countries, states, product categories, and shipping modes** to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Understand sales distribution across **countries and states**
- Evaluate performance by **category and market**
- Analyze customer shipping preferences
- Identify high-revenue and high-volume regions

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI**
- Power Query (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
- DAX (Calculated Measures & KPIs)
- Data Visualization & Dashboard Design

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

### 🌍 Sales by Country
- United States is the top contributor to sales
- Strong performance from Australia, China, France, and India

### 🗺 Sales by State
- High sales concentration in states like **Bihar and England**
- Clear visibility of regional performance differences

### 🏷 Sales by Category
- **Furniture** dominates total sales
- Category-level insights help in inventory and pricing decisions

### 🌐 Sales by Market
- APAC and LATAM contribute the highest share of revenue
- EU and US markets show stable performance

### 🚚 Sales by Ship Mode
- **Standard Class** accounts for the majority of sales
- Same Day shipping has the lowest contribution

---

## 📂 Dashboard Features
- Interactive slicers for:
  - Category
  - Market
- Drill-down analysis by country and state
